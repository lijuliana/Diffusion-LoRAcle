## Workshop facts (from https://interpscience.github.io/cfp)

- **Deadline: 1 September 2026** (extended from 28 August).
- **Length: up to 5 pages (short) or 9 pages (long).** References and appendices do not count.
- **Format:** ICLR or NeurIPS accepted for submission; camera-ready must be NeurIPS.
- **Double-blind.** The submitted draft must contain no identifying information, so compile
  `\usepackage{neurips_2026}` WITHOUT the `final` option. `final` is camera-ready only.
- **Non-archival**, posters with a subset selected for talks.
- Submission requires at least one reciprocal reviewer.

## Why this venue fits the paper

The call asks for measurement validity, falsifiability, experiments that rule out alternative
explanations, and pathways to replicable science, and states that interpretability work on non-LLM
architectures is in scope. The paper's control design, the finding that a feature ordering
established on a small clamped-recipe test inverts at the scale a reader operates at, and the
protocol derived from four failed runs all answer that call directly, and the subject model is an
image diffusion transformer rather than a language model.
